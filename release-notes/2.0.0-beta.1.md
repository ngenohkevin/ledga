## What's new
- Ledga has been rebuilt from the ground up, with a new look in light and dark.
- Home shows your M-Pesa balance on each line, what you owe on Fuliza, and what you've spent this week, month or year.
- Activity lists every payment with search and filters, and shows where your money went and who you pay.
- Categories can have any icon and colour, and the ones you track show month by month.
- Notifications for large payments and Fuliza, and a daily or weekly summary.
- Export & restore: a full backup file, a spreadsheet, and Android backup of your history.
- Updates arrive inside Ledga, with a beta channel if you'd like new versions early.

## Fixes
- Totals move on as new messages arrive, without reopening Ledga.
- A payment Fuliza covered keeps its own details instead of showing only Fuliza's message.
- Received payments show who sent them.
- Every payment is listed, not only the latest 200.
- The update banner shows whenever a new version is out.
